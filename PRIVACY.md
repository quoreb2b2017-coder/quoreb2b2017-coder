## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

| Where | What we collect |
|---|---|
| **Contact and capacity requests** on our Sites | Name, work email, company, role, phone (if given), and the program details you send us, such as asset, geography, titles, industries, volume and qualification criteria |
| **Newsletter subscriptions** | Email address, topics you choose, and your unsubscribe status |
| **Gated resources** (white papers, reports, case studies, webinars) | Email and the form fields shown, such as name, company, job title, country and answers to qualifying questions, and whether you agreed to marketing |
| **Partner and client relationships** | Business contact details of your team, invoicing details, program specifications and suppression lists |
| **Emails and calls with us** | What you tell us and our record of the conversation |

### 2.2 Information collected automatically

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| **Essential cookies** | Store your cookie choice (`hm_consent`, about 180 days on HealthMatics) and keep the site secure. Always on. |
| **Analytics** (only with your consent) | An anonymous visitor ID (`hm_vid`, about 400 days) and page-view events: page path, referrer host, device and screen hints, browser language and time zone, and approximate country, region or city from network headers. |
| **Marketing / attribution** (only with your consent) | A first-touch cookie (`hm_attr`, about 90 days) with landing page, time and UTM campaign tags. Turning marketing off deletes it. |
| **IP address** | Used for security and consent records. We store it pseudonymized: the last part of an IPv4 address is zeroed and IPv6 addresses are truncated. |
| **Google Analytics** (optional, only with your consent) | Where enabled, it runs under Google Consent Mode, with analytics storage denied until you opt in. |

We do not store full email addresses in cookies or analytics. If an email address appears in a URL, we keep only its domain.

### 2.3 Our B2B contact database

To deliver the Services, we maintain a proprietary US business-to-business contact database that we have built and refined over about nine years. It holds **professional** information such as name, job title, company, work email, work phone, company size, industry and business location. It may also hold the answers you give when one of our agents calls to verify your interest in a business topic.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information, such as health, financial account, government ID or biometric data, in this database.

## 4. Legal bases (EU, UK and similar laws)

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on:

- **Consent** for analytics and marketing cookies, and for marketing emails where the law requires it. You can withdraw consent at any time.
- **Contract** to provide Services and resources you request.
- **Legitimate interests** to run B2B outreach and our B2B database, keep our Sites secure and improve our Publications, balanced against your rights. You can object at any time.
- **Legal obligation** where we must keep or disclose information by law.

## 5. B2B outreach and lead delivery

5.1 **Contact by our team.** If your details are in our database, one of our agents may contact you by email or phone about a business topic, such as a resource from a technology vendor. Our outreach operates within the U.S. CAN-SPAM Act. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link.

5.2 **Verification.** If you agree to receive information from a vendor, our agent confirms your details and interest, and may ask about your role and buying plans (for example budget, authority, need and timeline).

5.3 **Delivery to partners.** If you confirm your interest, we deliver your business contact details and answers to the partner running that program, which passes them to its client, the vendor whose content or offer you asked about. They will then use your information under their own privacy policies.

5.4 **Opting out.** You can ask us at any time to stop contacting you or to remove you from our database. We will add you to our suppression list so you are not contacted again in future programs. Requests already passed to a partner are forwarded to it.

## 6. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information for money. We share it only as follows:

- **Partners and their clients**, when you confirm interest in a program (Section 5.3).
- **Service providers** that help us operate, under contracts that limit their use of the data. These include website hosting (Vercel), database hosting (Supabase), email delivery, analytics (Google Analytics, only with your consent), and our own tele-verification team.
- **Professional advisers** such as lawyers, auditors and accountants.
- **Authorities**, where required by law or to protect our rights, users or the public.
- **A buyer or successor**, if our business or assets are sold or merged, subject to this policy.

**US state privacy laws.** Delivering leads to partners at your request, and the optional marketing cookies described above, may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws, such as California's. You can opt out at any time by contacting us (Section 10) or by turning marketing cookies off.
