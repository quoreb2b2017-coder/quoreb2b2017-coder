## How a lead reaches you

```
Program spec  →  Source  →  Match & suppress  →  Tele-verify  →  Deliver
```

1. **Source.** Every record comes from our proprietary US B2B database, built and owned in-house over roughly nine years. Nothing is rented or resold from third-party brokers.
2. **Match & suppress.** Contacts are matched to your ICP, filters and target accounts. Your suppression lists are applied and records are de-duplicated.
3. **Tele-verify.** A live agent on our Pune team confirms each lead by phone, plus BANT and custom questions where the program calls for them. Records that don't clear are dropped.
4. **Deliver.** Clean records go out on your field map. Each one is traceable to its source, qualification and verification. Anything out of spec is replaced.
